Since the scanner already has the full line-text available per match (not just the isolated token), designed a narrow, deterministic, two-factor rule rather than attempting semantic analysis: (1) SHAPE -- looksLikeMultiWordIdentifier() requires camelCase with 2+ real capital-led lowercase-word segments, or snake_case with 3+ segments that are each purely alphabetic (2+ letters, no digits) -- rejects any digit-mixed or single-segment run, which is exactly the shape a generated secret/token essentially never avoids landing on; (2) POSITION -- isKnownCodeSymbolUsage() additionally requires the match to sit in one of exactly two source-code positions a real credential value structurally cannot occupy: a bare (unquoted) identifier immediately after '{'/','/'('/'.' or immediately before '(' (import list / call expression / property access / declaration), or the exact first quoted string-literal argument of a Supabase '.rpc(' call (this project's own established RPC-name-as-string pattern, already used identically by roster-parser and every other AI Edge Function).
