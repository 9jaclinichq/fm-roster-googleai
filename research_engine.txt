1. GIT BRANCH SETUP:
   - Create and switch to a new branch: `feature/universal-research-engine`.

2. DATABASE SCHEMA MIGRATION:
   - Create `supabase/migrations/12_universal_research_engine.sql` containing:
     a) `research_templates` table:
        (id, tenant_id [nullable - NULL for global templates, tenant_id for institution/group templates], created_by_user_id [nullable - NULL for system templates, user_id for individual custom templates], name, is_public [boolean, default true], organization_or_body ['WACP', 'NPMCN', 'ICMJE', 'CONSORT', 'STROBE', 'PRISMA', 'CARE', 'University_Thesis', 'Custom_Doctor'], specialty, study_design ['cross_sectional', 'cohort', 'case_control', 'clinical_trial', 'qualitative', 'systematic_review', 'case_series'], proposal_rubric [jsonb], dissertation_rubric [jsonb], referencing_style ['vancouver', 'apa7', 'harvard'], word_count_limits [jsonb], created_at, updated_at).

     b) EXHAUSTIVE SEED TEMPLATES:
        - 'WACP Family Medicine Proposal (v5.0, May 2025)'
        - 'WACP Fellowship Dissertation (v4.1, Aug 2022)'
        - 'NPMCN Family Medicine Fellowship Proposal & Thesis'
        - 'ICMJE / Vancouver General Medical Manuscript'
        - 'STROBE Cross-Sectional / Observational Study'
        - 'CONSORT Randomized Controlled Trial Protocol'
        - 'PRISMA Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis'
        - 'CARE Clinical Case Series & Case Report'
        - 'Standard MSc / MPH Academic Thesis'

     c) `research_workspaces` table:
        (id, tenant_id [nullable - allows independent doctors to use platform without institutional linking], workforce_id / user_id, title, study_design, template_id, pico_framework [jsonb], target_exam_date, folder_tree [jsonb], status ['proposal_draft', 'proposal_approved', 'data_collection', 'thesis_writeup', 'completed'], created_at).

     d) `research_chapters` table:
        (id, workspace_id, chapter_type ['proposal', 'ch1_intro', 'ch2_lit_review', 'ch3_methods', 'ch4_results', 'ch5_discussion'], chapter_number [0-5], title, word_count, content_text, section_scores [jsonb], ai_audit_logs [jsonb], updated_at).

     e) `research_correction_logs` table:
        (id, workspace_id, comment_source ['college_assessor', 'supervisor_round_1', 'supervisor_round_2', 'peer_reviewer'], section_topic, original_comment, action_taken, status ['pending', 'resolved'], created_at).

     f) RLS policies: Permissive anon SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE with tenant_id/user_id filtering at databaseService.

     * Load global public seed templates.

     * Edit section word caps, chapter titles, scoring rubrics, referencing rules (Vancouver vs APA7), and custom prompt rules.
